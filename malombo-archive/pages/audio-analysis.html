Timestamp 0:00

Repetition as propulsion

The piece begins through guitar ostinatos and interlocking pulse rather than harmonic exposition. Repetition does not stall the music; it creates the field within which attention starts to align, deepening the listener’s bodily orientation to the groove.

  • Pulse is sustained rather than redirected toward cadence.
  • Guitar and percussion operate as a shared rhythmic environment.
  • Repetition produces immersion before it produces interpretation.

Timestamp 0:58

Layering and accumulation

“Sangoma” grows by accretion rather than sectional contrast. Percussion intensifies around the repeating guitar figure, the guitar itself often behaves percussively, and pennywhistle phrases circle the texture in ways that resemble chant and invocation rather than melodic lead writing.

  • Layers accumulate instead of announcing a new scene.
  • Texture does the formal work that harmony often does elsewhere.
  • Duration matters as much as motif or theme.

Timestamp 2:25

Temporal suspension and trance effect

As the track continues, expectation of resolution begins to loosen. Tension is not organized through progression and release, but through duration and density. The result is a listening experience closer to ritual entrainment than conventional jazz soloing: attention shifts away from anticipation and toward immersion [9].

  • The cycle remains stable while sonic pressure deepens.
  • Small variations become perceptually magnified over time.
  • The recording preserves ritual logic inside public circulation.

Analytical Claim

Why this track matters

“Sangoma” demonstrates that Tabane’s music does not simply borrow ritual imagery. Its form is built through repetition, layering, rhythmic accumulation, and altered temporality, making the paper’s argument audible rather than merely descriptive.

Method

Listening as archival method

Close listening here follows structure rather than genre labels. The question is not whether the piece sounds “traditional” or “jazz,” but how it teaches the listener to inhabit a field of embodied repetition, density, and invocation.
