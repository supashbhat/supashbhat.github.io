About the researcher

Supash Bhat Music 170 Professor Davies

This archive extends the final paper, Malombo’s Spirit: Possession, Healing, and Modernity from Venda Ritual to South African Jazz, submitted on May 12, 2026. It keeps the paper’s argument intact while giving audio, visual, and historical materials a clearer public form.

The goal is not just to post an essay online, but to stage research as a navigable archive where ritual theory, apartheid history, close listening, and Philip Tabane’s modern performance practice remain visibly connected.

Project statement

The archive argues that Malombo does not mark a clean transition from sacred ritual into secular art. Instead, it shows how spiritual epistemology persists within modernity itself, carried through rhythm, repetition, invocation, and embodied listening.

Expansion Plan

Next additions could include more primary-source media, tighter timestamped annotations, archival recordings beyond “Sangoma,” and expanded work on how Malombo refuses colonial binaries between tradition and modern intellectual production.
